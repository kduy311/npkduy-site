Working Papers
Social Networks and the Spread of Labour Strikes
(with Assist. Prof. Huiyi Chen)
Work in Progress
This paper examines the role of social media and online networks in the diffusion of labour strikes in the United States. Using the Cornell ILR School's Labor Action Tracker enriched with social media engagement variables — Facebook likes, followers, and group members — collected via custom Python web-scraping scripts, we study how online visibility and network exposure shape the timing, spread, and intensity of strike activity. The analysis leverages a panel of over 20 million observations and exploits variation in social media engagement across labour union strikes.
Data collection: Facebook engagement indicators for each strike event in the Cornell ILR Labor Action Tracker, 2021–present.
Impact of Sustainable Mangrove–Shrimp Farming Innovations on Fish Farmer Welfare
Research conducted at GIZ Vietnam
Work in Progress
This paper estimates the causal impact of sustainable mangrove–shrimp farming innovations on the welfare of fish farmers in the Mekong Delta, Vietnam. Using a Difference-in-Differences design applied to a panel database of 300+ fish farmers, the study evaluates outcomes aligned with SDG 2 (Zero Hunger). Conducted in collaboration with the Deutsche Gesellschaft für Internationale Zusammenarbeit (GIZ).
